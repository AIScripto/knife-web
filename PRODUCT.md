## Users
1. Discerning collectors and knife enthusiasts seeking authentic hand-folded Damascus steel (raindrop, twist, ladder patterns) with premium exotic handle materials (stag antler, camel bone, stabilized burl).
2. Culinary professionals and passionate home chefs requiring razor-honed Japanese VG-10 core knives with superior balance and edge retention.
3. Outdoor sportsmen and hunters requiring full-tang hunting knives, gut hooks, and hand-stitched leather sheaths.
4. Heirloom and luxury gift givers valuing museum-grade craftsmanship, rapid California dispatch, and guaranteed 30-day returns.

## Product Purpose
White Hills Knives is a direct-to-consumer artisanal bladesmith atelier offering hand-forged Damascus steel pocket knives, hunting knives, chef knives, and Viking axes. The purpose is to provide heirloom-grade edged tools directly to collectors and culinary artisans at atelier-direct pricing with rapid domestic dispatch.

## Positioning
Authentic hand-forged folded Damascus steel (hundreds of folded layers of 1095 and 15N20 high-carbon steel, or VG-10 Japanese steel) combined with hand-carved natural handle materials, tested at the anvil and shipped fast from California with 1,700+ verified customer reviews.

## Operating Context
Users browse and evaluate knives online, inspect intricate Damascus water patterns, compare steel hardness (58-60 HRC) and dimensions, customize aesthetic viewing environments (multi-theme atelier lighting), and purchase with fast shipping and lifetime craftsmanship guarantees.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive blade inspector with technical specifications (steel layers, rockwell hardness, handle material)
- Full responsiveness across desktop, tablet, and mobile with high-contrast accessibility
